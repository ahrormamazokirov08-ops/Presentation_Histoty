Historical Evolution & Transition Slide 02 / 20

Historical Evolution: From Transition Economy to Benchmark Openness

A paradigm shift in national methodology: 1991–2016 vs. 2016–Present

First Phase: 1991–2016 (Cautious Transition)
  • Self-Reliant Model: Gradualist economic transition with limited engagement in external international evaluations.
  • Data Asymmetry: Official statistics were largely internal; international ranking agencies suffered from chronic lack of verified empirical data.
  • Currency & Market Barriers: Multiple exchange rates, state-controlled currency conversions, and high customs barriers limited cross-border ratings.
  • Perception Gap: International indices routinely penalized Uzbekistan due to non-reporting or conservative estimation models.
Second Phase: 2016–Present (Active Global Integration)
  • Strategy of Actions (2017–2021): Liberalization of currency market, tax code overhaul, and administrative deregulation.
  • Openness Doctrine: Adoption of international methodological standards (IMF SDDS, UN Open Data standards).
  • Benchmarking as State Policy: Presidential Decrees mandating state agencies to align legislation directly with index indicators.
  • Direct Institutional Dialogue: Active collaboration with World Bank, WIPO, Transparency International, and Heritage Foundation.
Key Takeaway: International ratings shifted from external scrutiny to an internal compass for sovereign modernization. 💬 View Speech Script
Institutional Architecture Slide 03 / 20

Legal & Institutional Architecture: Decree UP-6003 & The Council

Creating a unified, legally mandated mechanism for tracking, analysis, and accountability

Presidential Decree UP-5729 & UP-6003

Signed in June 2020, UP-6003 established a new mechanism of state control over priority ratings. It mandated individual roadmaps for each prioritized index.

Foundational Law
The Republican Council

Co-chaired by the Chairman of the Senate and Chairman of the Legislative Chamber (Oliy Majlis). Coordinates all state bodies, reviews quarterly ministerial reports, and resolves inter-agency bottlenecks.

Parliamentary Oversight
Single Portal & Research Institute

The establishment of ratings.gov.uz and the Institute on Problems of Legislation and Parliamentary Research to scientifically simulate policy impacts before implementation.

Digital Monitoring
Core Working Mechanism of the Republican Council:

Every single ministry and agency is assigned personal KPI responsibility for specific sub-indicators (e.g., Ministry of Justice for Rule of Law; Ministry of Economy & Finance for Economic Freedom; Statistics Agency for ODIN). Failure to improve in sub-indicators triggers parliamentary hearings.

Score Progression & Rank Elevation

Moved from "Repressed" status in the early 2010s to "Moderately Free" categories, demonstrating one of the largest multi-year point gains in Eurasia:

Critical Gaps & Execution Slide 19 / 20

Critical Gaps, Structural Challenges & Roadmaps

A realistic assessment: Navigating methodology constraints and bureaucratic inertia

Remaining Structural Challenges
  • Time-Lag in Methodologies: Global indices often reflect data from 18–24 months prior, delaying recognition of freshly enacted reforms.
  • State-Owned Enterprise Footprint: SOEs still dominate heavy industry, energy, and banking, requiring accelerated demonopolization.
  • Regional Khokimiyat Capacity: Disparity between central ministries in Tashkent and regional administrative execution in remote districts.
  • Informal Labor Sector: Continuing transition of informal shadow employment into official registered tax brackets.
Acceleration Roadmaps
  • Active Dialogue with Rating Agencies: Regular bilateral methodology workshops with WIPO, WJP, and Heritage Foundation analysts.
  • Sub-National Rating Dashboards: Implementing index benchmarks directly down to individual city and district Khokims.
  • Digital Real-Time Reporting: Integrating live API feeds between government registries and open international repositories.
  • Judicial Enforceability: Full codification of international commercial arbitration awards without local judicial resistance.
Strategic Principle: Transparency demands acknowledging weaknesses to engineer targeted solutions. 💬 View Speech Script
