Historical Evolution & Transition Slide 02 / 20

Historical Evolution: From Transition Economy to Benchmark Openness

A paradigm shift in national methodology: 1991–2016 vs. 2016–Present

First Phase: 1991–2016 (Cautious Transition)
  • Self-Reliant Model: Gradualist economic transition with limited engagement in external international evaluations.
  • Data Asymmetry: Official statistics were largely internal; international ranking agencies lacked verified empirical data.
  • Currency & Market Barriers: Multiple exchange rates and state-controlled currency conversions created perception gaps.
Second Phase: 2016–Present (Active Global Integration)
  • Strategy of Actions (2017–2021): Full liberalization of the currency market and comprehensive tax overhaul.
  • Openness Doctrine: Adoption of international methodological standards (IMF SDDS, UN Open Data).
  • Benchmarking as State Policy: Mandatory ministerial KPIs tied directly to sovereign rank improvements.
Key Takeaway: International ratings shifted from external scrutiny to an internal compass for sovereign modernization. 💬 View Speech Script

Critical Gaps & Execution Slide 19 / 20

Critical Gaps, Structural Challenges & Roadmaps

A realistic assessment: Navigating methodology constraints and bureaucratic inertia

Remaining Structural Challenges
  • Time-Lag in Methodologies: Global indices often reflect data from 18–24 months prior, delaying recognition of reforms.
  • State-Owned Enterprise Footprint: SOEs still dominate heavy industry, energy, and banking, requiring accelerated privatization.
  • Regional Khokimiyat Capacity: Disparity between central ministries in Tashkent and regional administrative execution.
Acceleration Roadmaps
  • Direct Dialogue with Agencies: Regular bilateral methodology workshops with WIPO, WJP, and Heritage Foundation.
  • Sub-National Rating Dashboards: Implementing index benchmarks directly down to individual city and district Khokims.
  • Digital Real-Time Reporting: Integrating live API feeds between government registries and open international repositories.
Strategic Principle: Transparency demands acknowledging weaknesses to engineer targeted solutions. 💬 View Speech Script
